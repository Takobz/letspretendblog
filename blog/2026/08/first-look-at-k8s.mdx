## What Is Kubernetes

Kubernetes, sometimes reffered to as K8s, is container orchestration platform, for deploying and managing containerized applications across different machines. 
Now that sound fancy with words like "conatiner orchestration platform" but what does all of that mean, for us to understand that we need to maybe tell a story...  

### What It Means To Be A K8s Cluster 

Let's say we have an application that shows movie reviews, usually the site is happy with 10 concurrent users at a time.  
Then a very interesting movie comes out, that a lot of people have been buzzing about then suddendly we see a traffic of 100 users all at once.  

Our server starts acting slow because our application can't serve 100 concurrent users, this problem can be solved in one of two ways; `Vertical or Horizontal Scaling`.
What does vertical or horizontal scaling even mean?
